01 / Why BIIC

A battery is more than its last reading.

Battery health is hard to see from a single dashboard or test. The evidence is scattered across impedance, cycling, temperature, operating history and manufacturing data.

BIIC is building a centre that brings those signals together—so each battery can be assessed for what comes next, with the reasoning and record to support the decision.

Starting with LFP cells. Designed around Indian operating conditions and customer use cases.

06 / Platform & models

Look inside the signal.

BIIC’s planned models bring electrochemical insight together with measured behaviour. Their performance will depend on validation across suitable data and use cases.

01

State of health

Health estimate from impedance, cycling, temperature and history.

02

Remaining useful life

Remaining cycles to a defined threshold, with operating context.

03

EIS fingerprint

Degradation progression and early-failure signatures.

04

Abuse risk

Risk indicators informed by chemistry, condition and charging.

05

Second-life value

Suitability and residual value for a defined next application.

METHOD NOTE · Physics-informed design aims to encode degradation mechanisms rather than treat a cell as a black box. Model outputs are not yet validated products.

08 / Roadmap

Earn the next capability.

A phased build lets the data and customer use cases shape the centre as it grows.

Phase 01 · Foundation

Build the core

Start with cell-level data and a useful first intelligence layer.

  • Cell cycling and EIS
  • Environmental chambers
  • Electrical abuse & thermal imaging
  • Battery data platform
  • Initial SOH / RUL models
Phase 02 · Customer-led

Expand with evidence

Add higher-complexity capability as validation and demand develop.

  • Module and pack testing
  • Vibration, shock and crush
  • Thermal-runaway facility
  • Gas analysis & advanced imaging
  • Second-life grading line
Phase 03 · Full centre

Close the loop

Connect the intelligence layer with circular services and materials.

  • Integrated circularity centre
  • Passport across battery life
  • Recovery-route intelligence
  • Materials platform exploration
  • Mobile lab under study
This is a public design roadmap. Timelines, commissioning and final capability are subject to validation, investment and customer demand.
